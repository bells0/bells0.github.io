About · updated September 2026

Building with evidence, in the open where I can.

I am wonderbell. I build practical AI workflows around commerce, research, creative production, and software delivery.

Current focus

My current work is an Amazon-first operating loop: investigate product opportunities, check supplier and cost facts, create Listing images and copy, then learn from real feedback. I use AI to make the work more capable and repeatable while keeping business decisions and external actions under human control.

PickScout Agent ↗ makes the research method public. Listing Optimizer and Image Factory are private tools used for focused production work.

How I build
  • Keep observed facts, estimates, recommendations, decisions, and completed actions separate.
  • Give agents explicit permissions and preserve the evidence behind consequential steps.
  • Keep workflows small enough to test against real use and explain after a handoff.
